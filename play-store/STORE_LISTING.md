# DreamCast — Store listing

Paste into **Grow users › Store presence › Main store listing**. Lengths are
within Play's limits. Avoid adding superlatives ("#1", "best") or claims the
app can't back up — Play rejects misleading metadata.

## English listing (en-US, default)

### App name (max 30)

```
DreamCast: AI Dream Journal
```

### Short description (max 80)

```
Record your dreams and uncover what they may mean with thoughtful AI readings.
```

### Full description (max 4000)

```
Every dream carries a message. DreamCast helps you hear it.

Dreams slip away within minutes of waking. DreamCast gives you a calm, private
place to catch them — type or speak your dream, and receive a thoughtful reading
of its symbols, moods and themes, drawn from the long traditions of dream
interpretation.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Record dreams by writing or speaking, the moment you wake
• Receive a reading that explores what your dream may be reflecting
• See the moods and themes in each dream at a glance
• Create a beautiful Dream Card to keep or share
• Keep a private archive of every dream you have recorded

PLANS
DreamCast is free to try, with one reading per week. Monthly plans unlock more:
• Lucid — a reading every day
• Mystic — up to 5 readings a day, with every key symbol explained and a
  reflection to carry into your day
• Oracle — up to 15 readings a day, with insights that connect each dream to your
  recent ones and close with a guiding question

Subscriptions are billed through Google Play, renew monthly, and can be cancelled
anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app
before you subscribe.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your dreams are yours. No ads, no selling of your data. Delete your account and
everything in it at any time from inside the app.

PLEASE NOTE
Readings are generated by AI for reflection and entertainment. They are not
medical, psychological or professional advice. DreamCast is intended for adults
18+.

Privacy Policy: https://chinjiesylvesternkeh.github.io/Dreamcast-star/privacy.html
Terms of Service: https://chinjiesylvesternkeh.github.io/Dreamcast-star/terms.html
```
