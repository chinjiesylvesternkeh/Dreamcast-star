DreamCast

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

DreamCast ("DreamCast", "we", "us") is a dream journal that uses artificial intelligence to offer interpretations of the dreams you record. DreamCast is operated by CHINJIE Sylvester, an individual developer based in Cameroon, who is responsible for your personal information. You can reach us at chinjiesylvestern@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the DreamCast Android app and its servers collect, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

InformationDetailsWhy we need it
Account detailsYour email address, your password (stored only as a one-way hash), and an optional display name.To create and secure your account and let you sign in.

What we do not collect

  • Payment card or mobile money details. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play; we never see your payment method.
  • Voice recordings. If you dictate a dream, your device's built-in speech recognition service turns your speech into text. DreamCast receives only the resulting text. That speech service is provided by your device or its maker (for example Google) under their own terms.
  • Location, contacts, photos, files, or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide DreamCast: create your account, generate readings and Dream Cards, and show your archive.
  • To apply your plan's limits and verify subscriptions with Google Play.
  • To review reports about AI-generated content and improve safety.
  • To protect the service against abuse, investigate errors, and answer support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your dreams to build advertising profiles.

3. Artificial intelligence

When you submit a dream, its text (and, on the Oracle plan, short summaries of your recent dreams, so the reading can find patterns) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the reading. A short description derived from the dream is sent to our image-generation provider to create the Dream Card image. These providers process the content only to return the result to us, under agreements that restrict their use of it.

Readings are generated automatically, may be inaccurate, and are offered for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, psychological or professional advice.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run DreamCast, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers that run our servers, database and image storage.
  • Anthropic (AI readings) and our image-generation provider (Dream Card images).
  • Google Play, to verify and manage your subscription.
  • Error-monitoring services, which may receive technical error details from our servers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or as part of a transfer of the service to a new operator, who would remain bound by this policy.

When you share a Dream Card, you choose the app and the people you share it with; that sharing is outside DreamCast.

5. International transfers

Our service providers may process information outside Cameroon, including in the United States and the European Union. We rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account details, dreams, readings and reports: until you delete them or your account.
  • Subscription records: for as long as your account exists, and longer only where needed for legal or accounting obligations.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • After deletion, copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app under Account › Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions to stop future charges.

8. Security

Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Access to production systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise these rights by emailing chinjiesylvestern@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

10. Children

DreamCast is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
