Delete your account

You can delete your DreamCast account and its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open DreamCast and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email (without the app)

Email chinjiesylvestern@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my DreamCast account". We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password hash and display name.
  • Every dream you recorded, with its readings, mood tags, headlines and Dream Card images.
  • Reports you submitted and your subscription record.

Deleted data is removed from our live systems immediately and from encrypted backups within 30 days. Server logs that may contain your IP address are deleted within 30 days. We keep nothing else, except where the law requires us to keep a record.

Have a subscription? Deleting your account does not cancel billing, because Google Play manages subscriptions. Cancel first in the Google Play app: Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › DreamCast › Cancel, or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
